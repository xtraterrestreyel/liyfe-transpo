For pharmacies

A dependable pickup agent for your patients who can't get to you.

Liyfe Transpo is a local delivery service in Champaign and Vermilion counties. Your patients hire us as their personal pickup agent. We aren't asking you to hire us, pay us, or change how you dispense.

How we work with your pharmacy

1

The patient authorizes us

Each customer e-signs a pickup authorization naming Liyfe Transpo as their agent. We record when and how it was signed, and they can revoke it any time.

2

The patient tells you

We ask every customer to add us to your authorized-pickup list, using your own form if you have one. You stay in full control of who you release to.

3

We pick up and deliver

Our driver arrives with ID and the patient's information. The bag stays sealed until it's handed to the patient at their door.

Our standards

  • Signed patient authorization on file for every customer, with a printable copy available on request.
  • Drivers carry company ID and verify the patient's name and date of birth at your counter.
  • Sealed bags are never opened and are delivered directly, the same day.
  • Signature or ID required at the door for controlled medications.
  • Patient information is kept private and used only for the delivery.
  • Copays are paid by the patient directly to you. We don't handle them unless the patient arranges it.
  • Undeliverable items come back to your pharmacy the same day.

Want to tell your patients about us?

If you'd like, we can list your pharmacy as one that knows how we work, and leave flyers you can share with patients who need delivery. We list a pharmacy only with its permission.
