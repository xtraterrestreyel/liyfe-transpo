Questions

Good to know

Who do you work for, me or the pharmacy?

You. When you sign up, you hire Liyfe Transpo and sign an authorization naming us as your authorized pickup. We aren't hired or paid by any pharmacy, and we're not a pharmacy ourselves.

What information does the pharmacy need?

Pharmacies usually identify you by your full name and date of birth, and sometimes your phone number or address. We collect these during sign-up and give them to the pharmacy only when we pick up for you.

Do I have to tell my pharmacy about you?

Yes. Pharmacies decide who they release prescriptions to. Call or stop by and say Liyfe Transpo is your authorized pickup, or bring them the printable authorization letter from your account. Some pharmacies have their own form, and we'll help you with it.

How does the copay work?

Your copay goes to the pharmacy, not to us. Most pharmacies let you pay over the phone before pickup. If you have no copay, just tell us. If you're not sure, choose "I need help" when you order and we'll call you.

Can you pick up controlled medications?

Some, depending on the pharmacy's policy. Tell us when you order, and we may need your signature or ID when we hand it over.

Can I cancel my authorization?

Yes, any time from your account. We stop picking up for you right away.
